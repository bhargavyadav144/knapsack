About & How to Play

Illustrative example of a 0/1 Knapsack Problem

Knapsack is an optimization challenge. Imagine you are a stealthy thief robbing a house with a backpack that holds a max weight capacity.

🎯 The Goal

Steal the most valuable combination of loot without exceeding weight capacity. You must calculate the mathematically optimal haul!

🎮 How to Play

1. Choose Your Mode:
  • Select a pre-set Level for a quick challenge.
  • Use the Custom Solver to input your own problem parameters.
2. Build Your Haul:
  • Click 'Add' on items in the Available Loot section.
  • Monitor your Score and Weight in the top-right HUD.
  • If the bag turns red, you've exceeded capacity! ⚠️
3. Get Stuck?
  • Click the 💡 Hint Bulb in the header for a strategic suggestion.
  • Use 'Auto Solver' in setup if you just want the math answer.
4. Review Strategy:
  • After submitting, use 'Review Comparison' to see a side-by-side analysis of your picks vs. the perfect mathematical solution.
